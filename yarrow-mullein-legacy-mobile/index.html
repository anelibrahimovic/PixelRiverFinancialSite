What happens next?
  1. Complete the form and carefully review all details.
  2. Save the ticket number shown on the confirmation screen.
  3. Wait for an employee to check the request and respond through your selected contact method.
  4. If nothing arrives after 3–5 business days, call Member Services and quote your ticket number.

There are no automatic updates, live ticket tracking or in-app staff replies.
